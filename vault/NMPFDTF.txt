TITLE: Vintage Delta Air Lines Boeing 747-100 Night Ramp Operations
DESCRIPTION:
A low-angle forward-three-quarters view captures a classic Delta Air Lines Boeing 747-100 parked at an airport gate jet bridge during night operations. The aircraft showcases its iconic upper-deck profile, classic "widget" livery scheme along the nose cone and cheatline, and nose-gear assembly standing over wet tarmac reflecting floodlights. Ground support equipment, including a heavy pushback tug and baggage carts, surrounds the aircraft under the dark night sky.
