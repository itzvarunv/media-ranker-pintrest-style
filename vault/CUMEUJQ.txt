TITLE: Space Shuttle Endeavour Transport Past Randy's Donuts Night
DESCRIPTION:
Space Shuttle Endeavour moves along public city streets on a heavy-duty multi-axle overland transport rig towed by a Toyota Tundra pickup truck at night. The orbiter spacecraft reveals its forward cockpit windshield, black thermal protection system tiles on the nose radome, "United States" lettering, and tall vertical stabilizer fin. In the background, street lights illuminate crowds of spectating pedestrians and the iconic giant Randy's Donuts rooftop sculpture.
