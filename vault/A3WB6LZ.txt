TITLE: American Airlines Boeing 747 Taxiing Past Vintage Airport Parking
DESCRIPTION:
A vintage side-profile shot captures an American Airlines Boeing 747-100 wide-body airliner taxiing slowly along an airfield perimeter fence. The polished aluminum fuselage features the signature red, white, and blue cheatline, "American" titles along the upper forward deck, and the classic "AA" eagle logo on the tail. In the foreground, several 1970s-era automobiles are parked along the airfield perimeter wall under a clear blue sky.
