DESCRIPTION:
A supersonic British Airways Concorde passenger jet ascends during takeoff against a warm, orange sunset sky over New York City. The aircraft is captured in high angle-of-attack rotation, highlighting its iconic ogival delta wing geometry, extended landing gear, deployed nose cone, and glowing fuselage navigation lights. In the background, the distinct twin towers of the World Trade Center stand silhouetted along the Lower Manhattan skyline during twilight.
