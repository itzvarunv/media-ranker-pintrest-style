NICHE: Aircrafts
TITLE: Air France Concorde Escorted by Dassault Mirage 2000 Fighters
DESCRIPTION:
A supersonic Air France Concorde passenger airliner flies in tight formation above coastal sandbars and turquoise tropical waters, flanked by four French Mirage 2000 military fighter jets. The white aircraft features a slender fuselage with a iconic ogival delta wing planform, droop-nose cone structure, and four underside-mounted Rolls-Royce/Snecma Olympus 593 turbojet engine nacelles. Below, camouflage-patterned delta-wing combat jet interceptors fly along the coastline, creating a high-contrast aviation scene capturing both commercial aviation history and military escort maneuvers.
