TITLE: Eastern Air Lines Boeing 727 Departure Over Manhattan Skyline
DESCRIPTION:
An Eastern Air Lines Boeing 727-200 tri-jet airliner climbs steeply after takeoff against the dense urban backdrop of New York City. The narrow-body aircraft displays its low swept wings, rear fuselage-mounted engines, center S-duct intake, and signature dual-blue cheatline livery along the silver fuselage. In the background, iconic architectural landmarks including the Chrysler Building and Empire State Building dominate the skyline under hazy daylight.
