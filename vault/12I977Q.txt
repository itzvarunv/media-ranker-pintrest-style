TITLE: Vintage SBD Dauntless Warbird Radial Engine Propeller Motion
DESCRIPTION:
A front low-angle view of a Douglas SBD Dauntless WWII naval dive bomber parked on a wet tarmac at night with its radial engine running. A long-exposure shutter captures a complete motion blur ring of the spinning propeller tips illuminated by warm ground lighting. Structural features include the air-cooled Pratt & Whitney radial engine cylinders inside the forward cowl, extended main landing gear anchored with wheel chocks, belly-mounted ordnance bomb casing, and riveted metallic airframe paneling.
