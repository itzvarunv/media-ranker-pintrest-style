TITLE: Pan Am Boeing 727 Tail and Engine Sunset Reflection Detail
DESCRIPTION:
A dramatic close-up over-wing perspective captures the aft fuselage and empennage of a vintage Pan Am Boeing 727 tri-jet parked on an airfield ramp during a vibrant sunset.
