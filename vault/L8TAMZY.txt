TITLE: Lufthansa Cargo McDonnell Douglas MD-11 Frontal Nose and Tail Engine Symmetry
DESCRIPTION:
A tight head-on perspective captures a Lufthansa Cargo McDonnell Douglas MD-11 tri-jet taxiing along an airport runway. The composition emphasizes the vertical symmetry of the airframe, showcasing the rounded nose cone, cockpit windshield glazing, illuminated nose gear taxi lights, and dual main landing gear tires. Rising directly above the fuselage crown is the large circular intake of the tail-mounted No. 2 General Electric turbofan engine, set against a warm, hazy horizon background.
